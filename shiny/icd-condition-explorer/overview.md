# ICD to Comorbidity Explorer

This Shiny application has a few simple objectives

1. Allow interactive exploration of the ICD codes within the [medicalcoder](https://www.peteredewitt.com/medicalcoder/) R package

2. Allow interactive exploration of the mappings between ICD codes and the
   conditions for each of the three families of comorbidities implemented in
   medicalcoder.


## Tabs:

* ICD: An interactive table of all the ICD codes within the medicalcoder package

* PCCC: An interactive exploration of the ICD codes mapping to the Pediatric
  Complex Chronic Conditions

* Charlson: An interactive exploration of the ICD codes mapping to several
  variants of Charlson comorbidities

* Elixhauser: An interactive exploration of the ICD codes mapping to several
  variants of Elixhauser comorbidities
